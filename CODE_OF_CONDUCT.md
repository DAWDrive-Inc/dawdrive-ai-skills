# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward, be useful, assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## Expected

- **Argue about the code.** Disagreement on technical judgment is the point of a security-focused repo. Bring the reasoning.
- **Be specific.** "This misses X because Y" is useful. "This is bad" is not.
- **Assume competence.** People come to agent tooling from very different backgrounds. Someone unfamiliar with one area is usually expert in another.
- **Accept a no.** Not every contribution fits the repo's scope. A declined PR isn't a judgment about you.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone
- Discrimination or derogatory remarks on the basis of any personal characteristic
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, or using an issue or PR as a delivery vector
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer via the email on their GitHub profile, or through GitHub's private reporting. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses scale with severity: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Deliberately malicious contributions are a permanent ban with no first warning.

Maintainers who don't follow this themselves are subject to it too.
